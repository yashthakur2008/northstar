01

Basic language

What is a stock ticker?

A ticker is a short code used to identify a publicly traded security. You type the code—not the full company name—into Northstar.

NVDANvidia

A company stock.

AAPLApple

A company stock.

MSFTMicrosoft

A company stock.

SPYS&P 500 ETF

A fund holding many companies.

QQQNasdaq-100 ETF

A technology-heavy fund.

BRK-BBerkshire Hathaway

A ticker containing a class marker.

Tip

Enter up to five symbols separated by commas, such as NVDA, AAPL, MSFT. Northstar removes duplicates and validates the format.

02

Hands-on walkthrough

Run your first research desk

  1. 1
    Choose one simple ticker

    Start with a company you recognize. Enter AAPL in the Security universe field.

  2. 2
    Choose the number of rounds

    Use four rounds for a first run. More rounds test more historical windows; they do not make the result automatically more accurate.

  3. 3
    Select “Run desk”

    The progress bar shows the current phase while live prices are validated and the agents examine the evidence.

  4. 4
    Read the conversation in order

    Market Data establishes the facts. Bull argues the positive case. Bear challenges it. Risk states the conditions that could invalidate either side.

  5. 5
    Read the PM scorecard

    Begin with direction, signal score, and confidence. Then open the analysis to inspect the reasoning, charts, evidence, and risks.

  6. 6
    Compare—do not blindly follow

    A result is a research screen, not an instruction to buy or sell. Compare it with company fundamentals, valuation, news, and your own risk limits.

03

The committee

Who is speaking in the live room?

M
Market Data

Confirms prices, dates, and the amount of usable history. If validation fails, Northstar withholds the result.

T
Technical

Measures momentum and volatility. It describes what price has done—not why the company moved.

B+
Bull

Builds the positive case and identifies evidence that could support continued strength or recovery.

B−
Bear

Challenges the Bull case with drawdowns, weak breadth, volatility, and clear failure conditions.

R
Risk

Applies safeguards and reduces conviction when evidence is incomplete, volatile, or contradictory.

PM
Portfolio Manager

Combines the debate into the final ranked screen. This is the verdict shown in the scorecard.

04

Reading the verdict

Understand the PM scorecard

#1Conviction rank

Ranks the submitted tickers against one another. First place is the strongest risk-adjusted setup in that specific run.

LONG / SHORT / WATCHDirection

Long indicates positive evidence, short indicates negative evidence, and watch means the evidence is inconclusive.

+42Signal score

Ranges from −100 to +100. Negative is bearish; positive is bullish. It measures evidence strength after a risk haircut—not expected return.

61%Confidence

Shows how much weight the screen assigns to its conclusion. Volatility, disagreement, and missing evidence reduce it.

$200.75Last close

The latest validated closing price. It may differ from a live intraday quote.

+2.9%Daily move

The change between the latest two validated daily closes.

Visual evidence

Charts, colors, and markers

Green/Bull lineEvidence supporting the positive case.

Up candleThe close finished above the open.

Down candleThe close finished below the open.

Line versus candlestick

A line chart connects closing prices and is easiest for beginners. A candlestick chart shows the open, high, low, and close for every period.

Hover markers

Move the pointer across a chart—or use the arrow keys when it is focused—to see the exact date, closing price, OHLC values, and volume.

Price is not the score

The price chart shows market history. The Bull–Bear chart shows how the agents evaluated that history. The two axes measure different things.

06

Use responsibly

What Northstar does not know

It does not guarantee future performance.

It does not know your finances, time horizon, or risk tolerance.

Price history alone does not establish valuation or business quality.

News, earnings, regulation, and unexpected events can invalidate a setup.

A high rank only compares securities included in the current run.

Always verify important information at the original data source.

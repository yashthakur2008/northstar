How to use the numbers

Useful statistics, with visible limits.

Current value

Latest validated price multiplied by the shares entered. It is not an executable brokerage quote.

Return vs. cost

Gain or loss relative to the average costs you provide. Holdings without a cost basis are excluded.

Realized volatility

Annualized variation in recent daily portfolio returns. Higher volatility means a less stable path, not automatically a bad investment.

Contributors

Best and worst positions by cost-basis return when available, otherwise by the latest session change.
